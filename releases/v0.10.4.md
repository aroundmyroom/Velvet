# Velvet v0.10.4 — multi-scrobbler support

## Send your plays to multi-scrobbler

Velvet can now send your plays to [multi-scrobbler](https://github.com/FoxxMD/multi-scrobbler),
a self-hosted app that forwards them to many scrobble services at once:
Maloja, Koito, Libre.fm, teal.fm, Rocksky, ListenBrainz, Last.fm and more.

- **How it works.** Velvet posts to multi-scrobbler's ListenBrainz endpoint
  source: *playing now* when a song starts, the listen after 30 seconds.
  multi-scrobbler passes them on to the services you set up there.
- **Admin.** Under **Admin panel → multi-scrobbler** you enter the server URL
  and a token. **Test connection** shows whether multi-scrobbler is reachable,
  its version and health, and whether the endpoint source exists.
- **Users.** Each user switches it on in the player under **Connectors →
  multi-scrobbler**, and can enter a personal token so their plays go to their
  own accounts. The page shows whether the last play was accepted, or that the
  token was refused.
- **Everywhere you play.** The Playing Now view has a multi-scrobbler badge
  next to Last.fm and ListenBrainz. The mobile and TV apps send too, Velvet
  Rooms send from the server, and Subsonic apps forward plays and now-playing
  with a separate **Forward to multi-scrobbler** switch.

### Why now

From 1 December 2026 a Last.fm API key only works when its owner has Last.fm
Pro. That applies to Velvet's own Last.fm scrobbling and to multi-scrobbler's
Last.fm client alike, so multi-scrobbler does not get around it. But every
other service keeps working without Pro, and multi-scrobbler reaches far more
of them than Velvet does on its own.

### Setting it up

multi-scrobbler is configured with environment variables or JSON files, not
in its dashboard, and its container needs a config folder it may write to.
`docs/multi-scrobbler.md` walks through every step: the folder rights, a
Docker compose example, the endpoint source, clients, connecting Velvet,
checking that it works and the common setup problems.

If a service is connected both in Velvet and in multi-scrobbler, it gets every
play twice: pick one route per service.

## Richer ListenBrainz listens

Listens sent to ListenBrainz now also carry the Velvet version, duration,
track number, album artist and the MusicBrainz recording ID when the file has
one, so ListenBrainz links them to the right recording more often. Subsonic
plays sent to ListenBrainz carry them too.

## Upgrade notes

- No database changes. New config section `multiScrobbler` (off by default);
  per user `ms-enabled`, `ms-token` and `subsonic-scrobble-ms`.
- A hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) loads the new version.

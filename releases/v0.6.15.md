# Velvet v0.6.15 — Fix: search results in disc folders play again instead of opening an empty album

A small player fix for search.

## Clicking a search result in a disc folder works again

In an **Albums-Only** folder, clicking a song in the search results opens
the song's album instead of playing the bare file. That broke for albums
split into disc subfolders, like `…/Voyage (Special Edition) (2CD)/cd2/`.
Velvet looked for an album at the exact `cd2` folder, while the Album
Library keeps the album one level up. The lookup failed, so Velvet fell
back to the album name in the song's tags, and opened an empty album view.
The song was neither played nor queued.

Now:

- A song in a disc subfolder (`cd1`, `cd2`, …) opens its album as intended.
- When no album matches at all, the song simply plays instead of opening an
  album guessed from its tags. That guess was the real trap in folders
  where the tracks carry different album names.

The **+** button wasn't affected; it always queued the song directly.
Details in [`docs/search.md`](../docs/search.md).

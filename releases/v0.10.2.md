# Velvet v0.10.2 — Home vibe, a new play button and a record behind the cover

## Home: keep the vibe going

Home opens with a new block under the greeting. Four album covers lie fanned out
like a hand of cards: the song you are playing first, then covers from your
shelves. Next to them: "More like *song*", its artist and a few similar artists.

- **Auto-DJ off:** one round play button starts Auto-DJ from that song. When
  nothing plays, it starts from the first song in Continue Listening.
- **Auto-DJ on:** the block says "Auto-DJ is keeping the vibe" with a pulsing
  dot, and has no button.

The shelves below show covers first: larger covers with rounded corners and a
soft shadow. Hovering lifts a cover and shows a round play button. Each shelf
shows exactly one full row, also after you resize the window. Shortcuts keeps
its current look.

Everything follows your theme, the colour taken from the album art, and your
skin.

## A new play button

The play/pause button is now a knob in a thin ring. While music plays, the ring
lights up in the accent colour with a soft glow, and every new song sends one
ring pulse outward. The large button in Now Playing works the same way.

Skins can colour the knob with `--play-bg` and `--play-fg`; the ring and its
glow follow `--primary`. The goud skin now has a gold knob with a gold glow.

## Playing Now: a record behind the cover

A vinyl record, a CD or a cassette sits behind the cover. While music plays it
slides out and turns (vinyl at 33⅓ rpm), with your cover as its label. On pause
it stops and slides back in; on the next song it comes out again with the new
cover.

In **Auto**, Velvet picks per song from what it already knows: folder names,
the album tag, the year, the length and the type of library.

- **Tape** for radio recordings, YouTube downloads, anything of 30 minutes or
  longer, and mixes (folders or album tags such as `Mixed by`, `Mix Club`,
  `Mixen`, `Jaarmix`, `Mix Cut`).
- **Vinyl** for vinyl rips, 12"/7" and maxi folders, files numbered like a
  record side (A1, B2), and songs from before 1988.
- **CD** for CD folders (`CD1`, `CDM`, `on CD`, `Disc 2`) and songs from 1988
  on.

"Radio Mix" or "Club Mix" in a song title is a version, not a mix, so it does
not turn a song into a tape. The full rules are in `docs/playing-now.md`.

Pick **Auto, Vinyl, CD, Tape or Cover only** under User Settings → Playing Now,
or click the cover in Playing Now to step through them. Your choice is
remembered and follows you to other browsers. Live radio, podcasts and
audiobooks keep the plain cover, and with reduced motion turned on in your
system nothing moves.

## Upgrade notes

- No database or config changes. A hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) loads the new look.
- If you wrote your own skin that styled `.play-main`, consider setting
  `--play-bg` and `--play-fg` on it instead, so the new ring and glow stay.

# Velvet v0.10.3 — Lyrics and Visualizer in Playing Now, plus two fixes

## Lyrics and Visualizer in Playing Now

Two buttons now sit right under the cover in Playing Now: **Lyrics** and
**Visualizer**. Nothing extra shows until you press one; a panel then opens
under the technical details.

- **Lyrics** come from Velvet's lyrics lookup. Synced lyrics scroll with the
  song, with the current line large and lit; unsynced lyrics show as a list
  you can scroll.
- **Visualizer** is Velvet's own visualizer, moved into the panel: Milkdrop,
  Spectrum, AudioMotion or VoltViz, with its usual controls on hover.
- **Full screen:** each panel has a full-screen button. Lyrics show large over
  the blurred cover, the visualizer fills the screen, and the song sits in the
  bottom corner. Esc or the same button brings you back, and full screen stays
  on when the next song starts.

Press the same button again (or the × in the panel) to close it, or the other
button to switch. The panel stays open from song to song while you stay on
Playing Now, and closes when you leave.

## Fixes

- **CD singles get a CD.** In Auto, CD singles inside a 12-inch folder could
  show a vinyl record: folders written as `_CDM_` (with underscores) or
  `CDM-1995` were missed. CDM and CDS now count however they are written, in
  the folder name or the album tag, and win over a 12-inch folder, a mix word
  in the folder name and the 30-minute rule. The word "vinyl" inside an artist
  name ("Three Drives On A Vinyl") no longer counts as a vinyl rip.
- **VU meters after a reload with Sonos.** After refreshing the page while
  Sonos played, the VU meters stayed still until you switched to web playback
  and back. They now move right away.

## Upgrade notes

- No database or config changes. A hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) loads the new
  version.

# Velvet v0.7.1 — Fix: artist pages for libraries organised by artist folder

A fix for artist pages in libraries where each artist has their own folder,
with the songs directly inside it or in album subfolders:

```
Music/Abba/*.mp3
Music/Black Sabbath/(1970) Paranoid/*.flac
```

## Another artist's albums on an artist page

On ABBA's page, the Albums tab could also list Black Sabbath's albums. In an
Albums-Only library, `Black Sabbath/` with its album subfolders becomes a
"series", and the artist page matched series names letter by letter: "Black
Sabbath" contains "abba". Velvet now only matches an artist as a **whole word**
in a series name. Real matches such as "Cerrone Discography" for Cerrone still
work.

## Empty artist pages

Songs that sit directly in an artist's folder, with no album subfolder, were
skipped when Velvet built the artist's profile. In the TV app that left the
artist page empty. They now show up as a release of their own.

## Song counts that were one too low

A song credited as "Afrojack feat. Eva Simons" only counted for Afrojack if
Afrojack had at least 10 songs in the library. In a smaller library it became
a separate artist, so Afrojack showed 5 songs while the folder held 6.
`feat.`, `ft.` and `featuring` credits now always count for the lead artist.
Your artist list may get noticeably shorter, because those one-off "feat."
entries fold into their lead artist; no songs are lost. "vs", "&" and "x"
collaborations are unchanged.

## After updating

The artist index is rebuilt automatically after the next library scan. To see
the fixes right away, use **Admin → Database → Rebuild Artist Index**.

All three issues date back to the first Velvet release and were not caused by
v0.7.0. Thanks to the user who reported them with a clear description of their
folder layout.

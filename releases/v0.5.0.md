# v0.5.0 — Velvet Keeps Secrets

This release adds no buttons. No settings. No menu entries. Nothing in the
interface admits that anything changed at all.

And yet.

## 🕵️ Four

Somewhere in Velvet, **four discoveries** are hiding in plain sight. Three of
them have been sleeping quietly in the interface for a while, waiting for
someone observant. The fourth arrives today — and it's our favourite thing
we've ever built.

We won't tell you what they are. We won't tell you where they are. But fair is
fair, so four whispers:

1. *Some words only appear when you play it loud.*
2. *Hold perfectly still. The party starts without you.*
3. *The needle knows more than it shows.*
4. *Some of us never stopped buying 12-inches.* 🆕

Corners are underrated. Music helps. That's all you get.

Found one? Keep it to yourself — or don't. Found all four? You already know
which one you'll keep coming back to. It even *sounds* the way it looks.

## Also in this release

- Fixed an artwork alignment issue in Playing Now on systems with reduced
  motion enabled.

## Upgrade note

Client-side only — a plain `git pull` (or pulling the new image tag) is
enough. Hard-refresh the browser after upgrading. Then go looking.

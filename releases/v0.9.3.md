# Velvet v0.9.3 — VoltViz, Music Assistant brain, rooms like the web player

## VoltViz visualizer

The full-screen visualizer gets a fifth mode, **VoltViz**, with more than 40
modern styles: shader scenes, particle fields, oscilloscopes, spectrum
displays and more. Step through them with the previous/next buttons or pick
one from the style list; your choice is remembered. Styles that need WebGPU
only show up in browsers that support it, and analysed songs hand their BPM
to VoltViz. VoltViz only loads the first time you select it, so the player
opens as fast as before. VoltViz is by Sander de Wildt (MIT). See
`docs/visualizer.md`.

## Velvet as Music Assistant's brain

Add Velvet to [Music Assistant](https://www.music-assistant.io/) as an
OpenSubsonic source and Velvet does the thinking:

- **Radio picked by Velvet's Auto-DJ.** Music Assistant's radio and "Don't
  stop the music" get a chain chosen like Velvet's Auto-DJ: each next song
  against the one before it (similar artists, BPM, key, genre, era, artist
  cooldown), no repeats or second versions of a song. Works without a
  Last.fm key too.
- **Top tracks** from your own play counts, blended with Last.fm.
- **Smart playlists** show up as read-only playlists.
- **Loudness**: every song carries its ReplayGain values, so Music Assistant
  levels the volume.
- **Synced lyrics** from `.lrc` files, the file's tags and lyrics the web
  player already fetched.
- **More detail**: BPM, genres, MusicBrainz IDs, the real audio format and
  artist biographies.
- Album lists (newest, most played and so on) now list whole albums and page
  correctly.

Plays made through Music Assistant are not counted in Velvet's history.

## Velvet Rooms: every output plays the same way

- **Auto-DJ in a room runs on the server.** When a room starts its last song,
  Velvet picks the next one itself, exactly like Auto-DJ in the web player:
  the same pool and scoring, all your Auto-DJ settings (similar artists,
  collections, rating, length, keyword and genre filters, BPM) and the same
  DJ state (artist cooldown, BPM, year and genre history). The browser hands
  that over when you put a room on Auto-DJ and gets it back when you return
  to the browser. Picks carry the *Auto DJ* tag in the queue. It no longer
  needs a browser tab: background, minimised or closed is fine, and it works
  the same when the music was started from Home Assistant.
- **A Sonos in a room keeps itself in sync.** Velvet measures where the Sonos
  really is in the stream and corrects the lead, quickly during the first
  minutes (when a Sonos shrinks its buffer in jumps) and every 30 seconds
  after that, also across song changes. The old *Delay* (0 to 5000 ms) is
  replaced by **Your fine-tune** (−1000 to +1000 ms, normally 0) for the
  Sonos's own sound delay; the automatic lead is shown next to it. Earlier
  delay values are not carried over, so set the fine-tune once by ear.
- **VU meters and visualizers move along** with a room's music.
- **Speakers come back by themselves** after a PC slept or Wi-Fi dropped:
  Velvet calls trusted speakers back every 30 seconds.
- Switching from a room to a Sonos or the server speaker continues at the
  same spot, with sound.
- The Sonos cover address always serves the cover of the song playing now.
- The "Playing in *room*" banner sits above the control row, by the queue
  button.

## Fixes

- Missing covers, artist photos and radio logos show the placeholder again
  instead of a broken image, and the on/off and delete buttons of scheduled
  radio recordings work. Both were blocked by the page's security policy.
- With Velvet Rooms switched off, the player no longer gets a 404: the room
  list answers "off". The Home Assistant integration understands both the old
  and the new answer.
- Embedded lyrics over Subsonic no longer come back as `[object Object]`.

## API

- New: `GET`/`PUT /api/v1/rooms/:id/autodj`; rooms show `autoDj`, queue
  entries `dj`.
- Sonos followers: `trimMs` replaces `delayMs`; rooms show `leadMs`.
- `GET /api/v1/rooms` answers `200` with `enabled: false` or `allowed: false`
  instead of `404`/`403`; other room calls answer `409 rooms_disabled` while
  Rooms is off.

See `docs/API.md` and `docs/API/rooms.md`.

docker pull ghcr.io/aroundmyroom/velvet:v0.9.3

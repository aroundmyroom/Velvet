# Velvet v0.3.12 — Search artist tracks shortcut

## Search — artist tracks shortcut

Finding all songs by an artist from the search view now takes one click instead of three.

Each artist row in the **Artists** section of the search results shows a small list icon on the right side of the row (visible on hover). Click it to open a flat track list for that artist immediately.

**How it works:**
- The track list is fetched by artist name + all name variants from the search response (e.g. `Bob Marley` and `Bob Marley & The Wailers`), so all recordings are included regardless of how the tag is spelled.
- **Play All** and **Append All** work directly on the resulting track list.
- The **back button** returns to the search results, preserving the query and scroll position.
- Clicking the artist name itself (anywhere except the icon) still opens the full artist profile with albums and bio — no existing behaviour changed.

Audiobook vpaths and albums-only scopes are excluded from the track list automatically, matching the behaviour of the artist profile's Songs tab.

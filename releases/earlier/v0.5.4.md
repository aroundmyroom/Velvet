# v0.5.4 — Auto-DJ Collaboration Intelligence

This maintenance release makes Auto-DJ smarter around duos, groups, and collaboration credits.

## Smarter Last.fm lookup

Auto-DJ now asks Last.fm for the credited artist exactly as written before trying fallback parts. That matters for real duo and group names: `Mel & Kim` is a known artist, while `Mel` and `Kim` are not useful substitutes.

When a credit includes a collaboration, Velvet now keeps meaningful groups together during fallback. For example, `Mel & Kim vs. Frantique` tries:

1. `Mel & Kim vs. Frantique`
2. `Mel & Kim`
3. `Frantique`
4. `Mel`
5. `Kim`

Fallback also continues when Last.fm returns names that do not map to artists in your library, instead of stopping with an empty usable result.

## Stronger repeat protection

Auto-DJ repeat protection now compares collaboration-aware artist keys, not only the full credit string. A recently played artist is still recognised when it appears inside a later collaboration credit, so `Eric Prydz` blocks an immediate `Eric Prydz & Steve Angello` pick.

This applies to the hard artist-repeat floor, the diversity score, and artist-history deduplication.

## Upgrade note

Server and client maintenance release — restart after upgrading. A plain `git pull` or the new Docker image is enough; hard-refresh the browser after upgrading.

# Velvet v0.7.2 — New: restore the previous queue after an album or song replaced it

## Undo a replaced queue

Clicking a track in an album plays the whole disc from that point, which
replaces your queue. One accidental click used to throw away a queue you had
built up.

Now, whenever the queue gets replaced, the **Up Next** bar of the queue panel
shows a **Restore previous queue** button (the curved arrow, in your accent
colour; hover over it to see how many songs it holds). One click puts your old
queue back and continues the song that was playing, from where it was. A song
that was paused comes back paused.

- Works after playing an album, playing a single song, and after **Clear queue**.
- Clicking another track in the same album doesn't overwrite it: your original
  queue stays the one to restore.
- Only wanted that one song? Restore your queue, switch the album's click mode
  to *single track*, and click the song: it's added to your queue instead.
- The previous queue is kept for the current browser session, and the button
  disappears once you've used it.

Details in [`docs/albums.md`](../docs/albums.md).

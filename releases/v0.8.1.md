# Velvet v0.8.1 — AI DJ: a set in phases

Velvet 0.8.1 teaches the AI DJ a request shape it could not express before:
a set that changes along the way.

## A set in phases

*"Start calm around 100 BPM for 15 minutes, then dance and disco from 100 to
120, then 30 minutes of club house a bit faster, then 30 minutes of dance,
then trance."*

In 0.8.0 that came back as one 25-song set from one tempo corridor — without
a single trance record. The brief had no way to say "first this, then that".
It now has **phases**: one entry per phase with its own genres or families,
tempo range, arc and length (minutes or songs). Everything else — era,
artists, exclusions, your marks and format wishes — applies to every phase.

Each phase is built as its own mixable set, in your order:

- its corridor **starts at the tempo the phase before it ended on**, and a
  steady phase is turned so it also **ends near where the next phase
  opens**;
- **no song plays in two phases**, and the admin's cap counts the whole set;
- a phase without a length gets the typical length of the phases that have
  one.

The result page lists the phases in playing order with what each one got —
songs, minutes, BPM range, arc — and the song list runs through them in that
order.

## A build between two tempos arrives

*"From 100 to 120 BPM"* used to stop wherever the +14% corridor ran out
(100 → 113), and the next phase opened with a 10 BPM jump. A build or
cooldown between two named tempos is now a journey laid out on evenly spaced
rungs from the one tempo to the other, each rung taking the best-scoring
song within half a rung of it. Five eight-minute disco mixes over twenty BPM
are 100, 105, 110, 115, 120 — the steps are what you asked for.

## Fixed in the mixing engine

- The gap filler refused to repair a wide tempo hole in the ladder (101 →
  113) when removing a song elsewhere would open a 3 BPM gap. It now judges
  a swap by the ladder it leaves behind, with the newcomer in place.
- The key and artist passes could turn a build back on itself at the end
  (… 129, 132, 130, 128): they only checked the size of a step, not its
  direction. A build climbs and a cooldown falls through those passes.
- A journey's corridor no longer keeps every song in a key compatible with
  one seed — over ten BPM of rungs that starved the top of the climb. Keys
  are matched transition by transition instead.

## For integrators

The brief gained `segments`; the build response gained `resolved.segments`.
Details in `docs/API/ai-queue.md`; the listener's guide is `docs/ai-dj.md`.

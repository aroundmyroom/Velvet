# Velvet v0.8.2 — Flow the queue

Auto-DJ and the AI DJ mix the songs *they* pick. Velvet 0.8.2 does the same
for the songs *you* queued.

## Flow the queue

A new wave button in the queue panel, left of Shuffle. Queue an album, a
folder, a playlist or a handful of songs by hand, press it, and every song
after the one playing is re-ordered so each flows into the next — taking off
from the playing song's tempo:

- the DJ's tempo ladder (no neighbour more than about 2.5% apart where the
  songs allow it);
- keys mixed on the Camelot wheel — the same key, one step round the wheel,
  or the relative major/minor;
- no artist twice in a row;
- songs without an analysed tempo spread through the list, not dumped at the
  end.

Nothing plays, pauses or skips, and which songs are in the queue does not
change. The toast says how many songs were re-ordered, the largest tempo step
and how many transitions are harmonic — the numbers the AI DJ shows — and has
**Undo**.

Up to 1000 songs are flowed in one go; songs the server cannot find are kept
at the end. The ordering takes a few milliseconds for a normal queue and
about 50 ms for a thousand songs.

## For integrators

`POST /api/v1/queue/flow` takes the file paths and returns a permutation of
the input indices plus `unplaced` and `stats`. No model call, no AI-assist
needed. Details in `docs/API.md`; the listener's guide is
`docs/queue-flow.md`.

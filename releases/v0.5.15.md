# Velvet v0.5.15 — Sonos Cede Fix

A regression fix following last night's Sonos casting rework (v0.5.12–v0.5.13).

## Sonos could seize control back and interrupt live playback

When the Sonos app takes control away from Velvet mid-session — a cede — the
web player is supposed to step back and stay hands-off until you press Play
again. It wasn't fully doing that.

The muted background tab kept resuming itself after ordinary network hiccups
regardless of the cede, and once it eventually reached the end of what it
thought was playing, the player pushed a fresh queue to the speaker anyway —
interrupting whatever was actually playing and replacing the upcoming queue
with an already-played track from earlier in the session. On screen, the
progress bar had already frozen at that point and never reflected any of it.

This has been confirmed live: real playback was cut off mid-song and replaced
with an old track, while the interface showed nothing had changed.

A cede is now genuinely inert. Nothing on the web player's side can push to
the Sonos queue again until you explicitly press Play or pick a track — the
same rule that was always intended, now enforced everywhere a push could
happen, not just in the obvious path.

This only affects sessions that were casting to Sonos on v0.5.12 or v0.5.13;
earlier releases are unaffected.

## Upgrade note

Restart the server after upgrading, and reload the player in your browser to
pick up the fix.

# Velvet v0.5.17 — Sonos Advance Race Fix

Another follow-up in the same area as v0.5.15 and v0.5.16, this time closing a
timing race rather than a straightforward logic gap.

## A natural track change could still restart itself, intermittently

If you were casting to a Sonos speaker, you may occasionally have heard a new
track begin correctly, then restart from the beginning about half a second to
a second in — with a matching jump visible on the waveform. Unlike the
previous two fixes, this one didn't happen every time.

The cause was two internal timers, both roughly 8 seconds long but started
from different moments, that could overlap. One is a safety net: if the
speaker doesn't confirm it moved to the next track within 8 seconds, the
player rebuilds the queue to make sure playback never gets stuck. The other
holds off recognising *which* track the speaker just reported for a similar
window after every cast, to avoid misreading a freshly-cast track's
still-settling status.

When a real, correct advance happened to land inside that second window, it
was quietly missed — which left the first timer with nothing to stop it, and
it rebuilt the queue on top of a track the speaker was already playing
correctly.

Before rebuilding anything now, the safety-net timer checks the speaker's
current state one more time and gives a real advance a genuine last chance to
be recognised. It only rebuilds if the speaker is, in fact, stuck.

## Upgrade note

Restart the server after upgrading, and reload the player in your browser to
pick up the fix.

# Velvet v0.3.11 — Starred smart queue append

## Starred view — rating-filtered queue append

A new compact toolbar appears above the song list in the **Starred** library view.

**How to use:**
- Click any star button (★ through ★★★★★) to instantly append all matching songs to the queue.
- The operator symbol to the left of the buttons (default **≥**) controls how the rating is matched. Click it to cycle through:
  - **≥** — songs rated *at or above* N stars
  - **=** — songs rated *exactly* N stars
  - **≤** — songs rated *at or below* N stars (only rated songs)
- The symbol highlights in the accent colour when a non-default mode (= or ≤) is active, making the current mode visible at a glance.
- A toast confirms how many songs were added; if no songs match the filter a brief "No matching songs" notice appears.

Available in all 12 supported languages.

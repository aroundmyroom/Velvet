# Velvet v0.5.12 — Sonos Casting Fixes

This release rewrites how Velvet drives a Sonos speaker. Casting kept the
upcoming queue only until the next track change, and tracks jumped back to
their start as they began. Both are fixed, and Sonos now reports what it is
doing instead of being polled for it.

## The upcoming queue survives

Every time Sonos moved to the next track, Velvet rebuilt the whole Sonos queue
from scratch. Rebuilding flushes the queue, so the Sonos app lost every
upcoming track and always showed the current song as track 1 of 1.

Sonos plays through the queue it was given perfectly well on its own, so
Velvet no longer touches it on a track change — it just follows along. The
queue is topped up as it plays down, without interrupting playback.

## Tracks no longer restart

The same rebuild also restarted the song the speaker had already begun. You
heard roughly a second and a half of the new track before it snapped back to
the beginning. The speaker now keeps playing undisturbed.

## Skips are identified exactly

Which track Sonos is playing is now read from the stream itself rather than
guessed by comparing the title and artist tags. A track queued twice, or two
files with no artist tag, can no longer be mistaken for one another.

Skipping to a track that is already queued on the speaker jumps straight to
it, so a burst of next or previous presses no longer rebuilds the queue once
per press. **Previous** pressed on the Sonos app is now followed too; it was
silently ignored before, leaving the player showing the wrong song.

## Sonos reports changes as they happen

Velvet polled the speaker every three seconds. Pauses, skips and track changes
made on the Sonos app now arrive as a push within a few hundred milliseconds.

This needs Velvet reachable by the speaker over plain HTTP, so it requires
`localHttpPort` to be configured — Sonos will not accept a self-signed HTTPS
callback. Where that is unavailable, nothing breaks: Velvet falls back to the
previous polling behaviour on its own.

## Smaller fixes

- Nudging the position a few seconds on the Sonos app is picked up instead of
  being discarded as ordinary playback drift.
- The player recovers its picture of the Sonos queue after a page refresh,
  rather than waiting for the next manual play.

## Upgrade note

Restart the server after upgrading, and reload the player in your browser so
it picks up the new client. Casting behaviour changes take effect on the next
cast; no configuration changes are required.
